Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026 · Fort Worth Veneer Suite · fortworthveneersuite.com

What we collect

  • Information you submit — your name, contact details, smile goals, and anything you write in our consultation request form or to our concierge chat assistant.
  • Usage data — basic technical information collected automatically when the site is served (such as pages viewed and device type).

Please do not submit detailed medical or health information through this website or the chat assistant — clinical matters are discussed privately at your consultation. This website is not a medical record system.

Our virtual smile consult is provided by SmileViz on its own platform; photos and details you submit there are handled under SmileViz's own terms in addition to our practice's care of your case.

How we use it

We use what you submit to respond to you, arrange consultations, and communicate about your care. We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it for us

  • Formspree delivers form and chat submissions to our practice email.
  • Google Workspace provides our practice email.
  • Anthropic processes concierge chat conversations to generate responses.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub host and serve this website securely.
  • SmileViz operates the virtual consultation platform.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to records we're required to keep). Texas residents have these rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. Requests: admin@fortworthdentist.com or 817-926-1300.

Children

This website is intended for adults. Consultation requests for minors should be made by a parent or guardian.

Changes and contact
